Free to use. Android 9 or newer. Premium is optional.

Beautiful, and quick on modest phones

Everything is built to look like glass without feeling heavy. A lite glass mode keeps things smooth on phones with 4 GB of RAM or less.

Depth clock

The time sits inside your wallpaper, behind its subject. Depth is free on the Tideline wallpaper; Premium brings it to any wallpaper and any clock.

Frosted glass

Glass panes, a leaning dock and smooth page transitions. Light, dark and Amoled themes.

Icons your way

Icon styles, installed icon packs, and sizes from Small to Huge.

Wallpapers

Live wallpapers, or your own photo with adjustable blur and dim.

Glass widgets

Clock, weather, calendar, month view, battery, system and music widgets, plus any Android widget you already use.

An organised drawer

Suggestions, fast search, automatic groups, folders, hidden apps and renamed apps. Ready-made folders on a new phone.

Gestures & Control Centre

Swipes and pinches for your own shortcuts, a glass Control Centre, and double-tap to lock.

Share your setup

Share a picture of your home screen in one tap. Private widgets stay hidden unless you choose to show them.
