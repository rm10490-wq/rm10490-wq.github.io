Launcher for Android

A home screen built around your wallpaper.

The clock stands behind the subject of your photo, so your wallpaper wraps around the time. Frosted glass panes, a leaning dock, live wallpapers and icon packs, with no account and no tracking.

Get it on Google Play See what it does

Free to use. Android 9 or newer. Premium is optional.

Beautiful, and quick on modest phones

Everything is built to look like glass without feeling heavy. A lite glass mode keeps things smooth on phones with 4 GB of RAM or less.

Depth clock

The time sits inside your wallpaper, behind its subject. Depth is free on the Tideline wallpaper; Premium brings it to any wallpaper and any clock.

Frosted glass

Glass panes, a leaning dock and smooth page transitions. Light, dark and Amoled themes.

Icons your way

Icon styles, installed icon packs, and sizes from Small to Huge.

Wallpapers

Live wallpapers, or your own photo with adjustable blur and dim.

Glass widgets

Clock, weather, calendar, month view, battery, system and music widgets, plus any Android widget you already use.

An organised drawer

Suggestions, fast search, automatic groups, folders, hidden apps and renamed apps. Ready-made folders on a new phone.

Gestures & Control Centre

Swipes and pinches for your own shortcuts, a glass Control Centre, and double-tap to lock.

Share your setup

Share a picture of your home screen in one tap. Private widgets stay hidden unless you choose to show them.

GlassHour

See today's time in the apps you open from GlassHome: a total, your top three apps, a friendly healthy-usage line and an optional daily goal. It never blocks anything, needs no usage-access permission, and nothing leaves your phone.

Fluid motion, everywhere

Apps open and close with Expand, Liquid, Zoom or Recede. Pages turn with Slide, Fade, Flip, Spatial, Parallax or Depth. The drawer slides and fades, and search grows out of the button you tapped. Speed is yours to set.

A widget strip that stays tidy

Your widgets sit in one swipeable strip with tag chips (All, Weather, Tools, Media...) to filter them. Resize any card, add your own tags, or add another app's widget. Free for everyone.

Discover wallpapers

Browse thousands of high-resolution wallpapers online, by category, and apply one in a tap. Included in the free version.

Made to be customised

A closer look at what you can change. Tap a poster to see it full size.
